How to Get to Amedzofe From Accra Travel to Ho by bus (about 3–4 hours). From Ho, take a local vehicle (trotro or taxi) to Amedzofe (about 1 hour). From Hohoe: It takes about 30–40 minutes to get to Amedzofe by road. Transportation Options: Private car/taxi (best for comfort and access) Trotros (shared minivans from Ho or Hohoe to Kpedze/Amedzofe) Motorbikes (local options for short rides up the hills)

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is during the dry season (November–March) when the weather is cool and clear. The rainy season (April–October) makes the landscape lush but can make hiking trails slippery.

Location: Tafi Atome, Volta Region, Ghana Coordinates: 7.1004° N, 0.4561° E Closest Major Town: Hohoe (about 20–30 minutes away by car) Region: Volta Region (South-Eastern Ghana) Take the Accra–Tema Motorway. Join the Akosombo Road (N2) heading north. Continue through Atimpoku and Akosombo (cross the Adomi Bridge). Drive toward Ho (the Volta regional capital). From Ho, head to Hohoe via Fume, Kpeve, and Have. Before Hohoe, take a turn toward Tafi Atome at Logba Alakpeti. 🚕 From Hohoe, you can take a taxi or trotro to Tafi Atome (about 25 minutes )
