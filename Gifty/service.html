Tafi Monkey Sanctuary is a community-based ecotourism site located in the Volta Region of Ghana. It is home to hundreds of sacred Mona monkeys, which live in harmony with the local people. The sanctuary is a must-visit destination for nature lovers, wildlife enthusiasts, and those interested in cultural heritage.

History

The Mona monkeys in Tafi-Atome are considered sacred by the Ewe people of the area.The sanctuary was established in 1996 as a conservation project to protect the monkeys and promote eco-tourism.The local community believes the monkeys are messengers of the gods, so they do not harm them.The initiative has provided income and development for the community through tourism. The sanctuary is home to the Mona monkey (Cercopithecus mona), a species found in West Africa.These monkeys are playful, friendly, and used to human interaction.They live in small family groups and are seen swinging from trees or coming down to be fed.Visitors can feed the monkeys bananas, which they gently take from your hands.

Best Time to Visit,Early morning (6 AM – 9 AM) or late afternoon (4 PM – 6 PM) for the best chance to see active monkeys.Dry season (November – March) offers easier hiking conditions.
